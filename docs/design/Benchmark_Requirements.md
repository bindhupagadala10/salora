# Benchmark Requirements

## Objective

The benchmark must demonstrate genuine domain adaptation rather than task adaptation.

---

## Mandatory Requirements

### R1

Same NLP task across all datasets.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R2

Same prediction objective.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R3

Same output label space.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R4

Publicly available datasets.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R5

Well-established in research literature.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R6

Sufficient dataset size for transformer fine-tuning.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R7

Different semantic domains.

Examples

General

Scientific

Legal

Biomedical

Customer Support

Financial

---

### R8

Natural justification for domain adaptation.

The benchmark should answer:

Why would an organization adapt a general model to this domain?

---

### R9

Compatible with PEFT methods.

Status

Mandatory

---

### R10

Easy to reproduce.

No proprietary datasets.

No licensing restrictions.

No synthetic benchmark.
