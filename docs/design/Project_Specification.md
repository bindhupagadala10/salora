# Project Specification

## Project Name

SALoRA: Shift-Aware Layer-wise Parameter Allocation for Parameter-Efficient Domain Adaptation

---

## Primary Goal

Publish one journal paper in a Q3-indexed venue.

Publication quality is prioritized over ambitious novelty.

---

## Success Criteria

The project is considered successful if:

- The method is reproducible.
- The experiments are rigorous.
- Strong PEFT baselines are included.
- Reviewer criticisms are addressed.
- The paper is accepted by a Q3 journal.

---

## Out of Scope

The following are NOT goals:

- Achieving SOTA.
- Publishing at NeurIPS.
- Reinventing LoRA.
- Creating a new transformer architecture.
- Proving new deep learning theory.

---

## Research Philosophy

We investigate whether representation drift is a useful engineering signal for adaptation planning.

We do not assume this relationship beforehand.

The paper is empirical first, theoretical second.
